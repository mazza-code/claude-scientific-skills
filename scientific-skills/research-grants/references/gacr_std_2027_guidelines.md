# GACR Call Profile: Standard Projects 2027 (`GACR_STD_2027`)

## Profile Metadata

- Agency: Czech Science Foundation (GACR)
- Group/call: Standard Projects 2027
- Profile ID: `GACR_STD_2027`
- Primary language for proposal content: English
- Scientific positioning: basic research with high-quality international outputs

## Official Source Documents Used

This profile is derived from the official call documents in `call_documentation/`:

1. `TD_STD_2027.pdf` (Tender Document)
2. `Practical_information_STD_2027_ENG.pdf`
3. `checklist_STD_2027_ENG.pdf`

If any wording differs, the official Czech tender text prevails.

## Timeline (This Specific Call)

- Tender deadline: 31 March 2026
- Evaluation period: 1 April 2026 to 26 November 2026
- Expected project launch: 1 January 2027
- Project duration: 2 or 3 years
- Expected completion: 31 December 2028 or 31 December 2029

## Submission Channel And Process

- Proposal forms are completed through GRIS.
- Submission is via data mailbox workflow in accordance with tender instructions.
- Proposal content is fixed after tender deadline (no post-deadline amendments).
- Material changes to core proposal identity (organization/applicant/aims/subject) can cause disqualification.

## Proposal Package Structure

The proposal consists of:

- Part A: Basic identification + abstract + scientific aim
- Part B: Funding totals + itemized costs + financial justification
- Part C1: Scientific justification file (uploaded PDF)
- Part C2: Expected outputs description
- Part D1: CV file (uploaded PDF)
- Part D2: Bibliography and track record
- Part E: Other projects and overlap disclosures

## Hard Formatting And Length Constraints

### Part A

- Abstract in English, max 1,100 characters including spaces.
- Scientific aim in English, max 300 characters including spaces.

### Part C1 (Uploaded File)

- External PDF uploaded via GRIS.
- Maximum file size: 6 MB.
- Maximum length: 10 A4 pages.
- Font/spacing: standard 11 pt, single spacing.

### Part D1 (Uploaded File)

- External PDF uploaded via GRIS.
- Maximum file size: 1 MB.
- Maximum length: 2 A4 pages for applicant, plus up to 2 additional pages for each co-applicant.
- Font/spacing: standard 11 pt, single spacing.

## Required Content (C1)

C1 must include:

1. State of knowledge and applicant contribution to date
2. Project substance, timeliness, aims, methods, phases, schedule, and linkage to expected outputs
3. Risk identification, intensity/probability, and mitigation
4. International collaboration scope/content (if planned)
5. Co-organization rationale and contribution (if involved)
6. Team/institution readiness and infrastructure
7. Team composition, roles, and expected workloads
8. Research data handling and data management approach (DMP commitment)
9. Potential benefits and future application potential
10. Communication plan for users/public, including GACR acknowledgement
11. References/literature used

## Expected Outputs (C2)

Expected outputs are described with emphasis on quality, not quantity. Recognized output families include:

- Jimp (WoS journal with non-zero impact factor)
- Jsc/Jost (specialized journals)
- B (monograph)
- C (chapter in monograph)
- D (proceedings article in Scopus/WoS conference index)

## D1/D2 And Part E Requirements

### D1

- Structured professional CV
- Activities in the project field over last 5 years
- Optional explanation of career gaps/productivity drops

### D2

- 10-year track record summary (with career-break adjustments where applicable)
- Five significant results with contribution and citation context
- Total citations and H-index information
- Supervision, teaching, invited talks, major awards, key prior projects, panel memberships

### Part E

Must include complete information on:

- Current and applied GACR projects
- Last 5 years of GACR participation
- Similar-topic projects from other providers (including foreign)
- Role, funding, FTE, and relationship to submitted proposal

Incomplete Part E information can cause disqualification.

## Allowed Annexes Only

Only the annex categories listed in the tender are allowed (for this call profile), including:

- Required special authorizations/licenses
- List of unsuitable foreign reviewers with justification
- Procurement quote package where required by thresholds
- Power of attorney (if organization lacks own data mailbox access)
- Letter of Intent for international cooperation/foreign institutional commitment

Any extra annex category not permitted by the tender can trigger disqualification.

## Budget Rules And Numeric Compliance

### Financial Syntax

- Financial amounts must be rounded to nearest thousand CZK in proposal forms.

### Personnel

- Zero workload (FTE) is not allowed.
- Recommended minimum annual FTE:
  - Applicant: 0.20
  - Co-applicant: 0.10
- Sum of GACR-supported project workloads for one employee must not exceed 1.00.
- Salary support cap: CZK 65,000/month (prorated by FTE).
- Work-agreement remuneration cap: CZK 400/hour.
- Planned milestone bonuses cannot be treated as standard salary basis for eligible personnel costs.

### Investment And Overheads

- Investment costs (tangible fixed assets) must not exceed 10% of approved costs base.
- Overheads/indirect costs may be at most 20% of approved direct cost base (as defined by tender formulas).
- If overhead not requested in proposal, it cannot be added later.

### Procurement Threshold Triggers

For high-value acquisitions, tender requires pre-justification and quote documentation according to thresholds stated in the call (including aggregate single-supplier threshold conditions).

## Evaluation Criteria (Scientific + Cost)

### Scientific Quality

- Clarity and significance of scientific aims
- Methodological adequacy and schedule quality
- Team competence/roles/workload adequacy (including student/postdoc involvement)
- Expected quality/usefulness of outputs
- Value of international cooperation (where relevant)
- Prior project performance/compliance history (where applicable)

### Cost Evaluation

- Appropriateness of costs/workload relative to aims and outputs
- Legitimacy of individual cost items
- Funding proportion appropriateness
- Compliance with eligible-cost definitions

## "Poor-Quality" Risk Category

For this call family, severe scientific defects can label a proposal as poor-quality, including:

- No clear knowledge gap
- Unclear hypothesis or no verification method
- Inappropriate methodology for stated hypothesis
- Proposed aims/outputs not achievable with proposed research/data plan

This classification can have consequences for submissions in following calls, so drafts must explicitly guard against these failure modes.

## Disqualification Triggers To Explicitly Check Before Finalization

- Not fully in required language
- Missing required proposal part(s)
- Wrong format/length/file-size for C1/D1
- Incomplete/incorrect identification data
- Incomplete Part E disclosure
- Financial format errors (currency/unit/rounding or internal math inconsistencies)
- Non-permitted annexes
- Contradictory/false information

## Recommended Generation Workflow In This Profile

Use this order:

1. Build compliance skeleton for A/B/C1/C2/D1/D2/E.
2. Draft C1 narrative with explicit mapping to evaluation criteria.
3. Build budget/FTE consistency table and check numeric caps.
4. Draft D1/D2 from role-specific evidence.
5. Run pre-submission checklist.
6. Only then polish language and style.

## Mandatory Output Contract For `GACR_STD_2027`

Any final deliverable generated under this profile must include:

1. Draft text for requested section(s)
2. Explicit constraints check (page/size/language/form)
3. Budget/FTE cap check (if budget content included)
